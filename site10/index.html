The Inn

Built in 1947. Restored in 2018. Unchanged in spirit.

The Saltgrass was a fishing camp before it was an inn. We bought it because we wanted a place to stay on the Gulf that didn't feel like a product. We still live on the property. You'll probably meet us at breakfast.
